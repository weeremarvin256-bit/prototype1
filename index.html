How it works

From your garden to your pocket in four steps

You-Farm connects growers and buyers directly, so no middlemen take a cut and nobody waits for their money.

1

Sign up & list your harvest

Farmers, buyers and transporters all register the same simple way. Farmers then post their crop, quantity and location, even by USSD.

2

Get graded & matched

Your produce is verified and graded, then matched with buyers who offer a fair price.

3

Choose your delivery

Pick a freelance transporter or a registered courier service, whichever suits you.

4

Get paid securely

The buyer's payment is held in escrow and released straight to your Mobile Money on delivery.

Delivery

Your harvest, delivered your way

Farmers and buyers choose how their produce travels. Transporters join You-Farm by registering the same way farmers and buyers do.
